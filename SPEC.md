# Todo for My Mom

## Who · What · Why · When · Where
- Who: My Mom.
- What: Do Groceries, Do Trading, and Make Dinner.
- Why: She does not usually store her tasks.
- When: In the morning and in the afternoon.
- Where: On her phone, using one hand, at home or in a busy shop.

## The 4 parts
- Data: Tasks such as Do Groceries, Do Trading, and Make Dinner.
- Display: A clear list that is easy to use on a phone with one hand.
- Actions: Add a task in five seconds.
- Memory: Keep tasks after a page reload.

## Done when
- [ ] Mom can add a task in five seconds.
- [ ] The app is usable on a phone with one hand.
- [ ] Tasks are still there after a reload.
